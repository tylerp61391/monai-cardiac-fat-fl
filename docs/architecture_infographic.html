3 Pericardium Sac DeepHeartSeg ONNX

2-stage CNN (~0.45s GPU) strictly isolating epicardium from mediastinal fat:

  • • Step 3A (Localization): Downsampled grid (64×128×128) locates 3D cardiac ROI.
  • • Step 3B (Pericardium): Cropped ROI (112×128×128) predicts sac + binary_fill_holes.
  • • Thread Control & Parity: intra=2, inter=1; 100.0% equivalence to MGH frozen weights.

  • • PVAT Dilation: 10.0 mm radial EDT cuff around LAD, LCX, RCA; union forms PVAT_Total.
